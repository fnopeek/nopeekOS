# Hardware

nopeekOS runs on real machines, and every driver is ported from Linux with
the device on the desk. Sponsor money goes into test hardware, and every
purchase is listed here.

## Runs today

| Device | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Intel NUC 11 Pro (NUC11TNKi3, Core i3-1115G4, Tiger Lake) | i915 GPU driver, Intel igc Ethernet, VT-x microVM |
| Lenovo IdeaPad Flex 5 14ALC7 (AMD Ryzen 5 5500U, Lucienne) | amdgpu driver, RTL8822CE WiFi, I2C-HID touchpad, battery, AMD-V microVM |
| QEMU | the easiest way to try it |
